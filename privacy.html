Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026. This policy covers Ecomi, the web application at app.ecomi.app and this website at ecomi.app.

Who we are

Ecomi is operated from the Netherlands. For anything in this policy, including requests to see or delete your data, write to support@ecomi.app. We are the data controller for your account data, and a processor acting on your instructions for the store data you connect.

What Ecomi is

Ecomi is a reporting and product-sourcing tool for online shops. You connect accounts you already own, Ecomi reads them, and it shows you your own figures. It is a multi-tenant service: each account sees only the stores belonging to it, and no customer can see another customer's data.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your name, username and email address.
  • A hash of your password. We never store the password itself and cannot recover it.
  • Session records, which include the IP address and browser you signed in from, so that you can see and end your own sessions.

From Google, once you connect it

  • Google Ads — campaign, ad group and product-level performance figures: cost, clicks, impressions and conversions.
  • Google Merchant Center — the status of your own product listings, so that disapprovals can be seen and corrected.
  • The OAuth tokens that allow those reads, and the identifiers of the accounts you selected.

From Shopify, once you connect it

  • Your products, variants, prices and inventory.
  • Your orders and refunds. Order records can contain customer details such as a name, address and email address, because Shopify returns them with the order.

From public competitor pages

If you use Market radar or the importers, Ecomi reads publicly available product pages from the stores you choose to track. It reads the public product catalogue only. It does not sign in anywhere, and it collects no personal data from those stores.

Operational records

Request timings, error records and the cost of the AI calls your account makes, so that the service can be kept fast and its running cost understood.

How Google data is used

Limited Use. Ecomi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Concretely, data read from Google is used only to:

  • show you your own advertising spend and performance beside your revenue;
  • calculate your profit after advertising cost;
  • show the status of your own Merchant Center listings.

It is never:

  • sold, rented or shared with any third party;
  • used for advertising, profiling or any form of targeting;
  • used to train, fine-tune or improve any AI or machine-learning model;
  • combined with another customer's data;
  • read by a human, except where you ask us to look at a specific problem, or where the law requires it.

Ecomi's access to Google is read-only. It does not create, change, pause or delete anything in your Google Ads or Merchant Center account.

AI processing, and what is excluded from it

Ecomi uses a large language model, provided by Anthropic, to write product titles and descriptions, to classify garment types and categories, and to translate colour and size names into your shop's language.

What is sent to that model:

  • product titles and descriptions from the supplier or competitor pages you choose to import;
  • product titles from your own catalogue, for classification;
  • colour and size names, for translation.

No Google data is sent to any AI model. Your Google Ads figures and Merchant Center status are never included in a prompt. Neither are your orders, your customers' details, or your revenue.

Anthropic processes this content to return an answer and does not use it to train its models.

Where it is stored, and who can reach it

  • Data is held in a private PostgreSQL database that only the application can reach. It is not exposed to the internet.
  • Access tokens are encrypted before they are written, and are never shown in the interface or written to logs.
  • All traffic to and from the application uses HTTPS.
  • The application is reachable only after signing in, and every query is scoped to the account making it.
  • Hosting and the database are provided by Railway. Administrative access is limited to Ecomi's operator.

Who else processes it

ServiceWhat it handles
Railway Hosting and the database. All stored data sits here.
Anthropic The AI model, for the product text described above. No Google data, no order data.
Google The source of the advertising and listing data you connect.
Shopify The source of the order and product data you connect.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics trackers or data brokers, and we do not sell data to anyone.

Cookies

Ecomi sets one cookie: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It contains no personal data and is not used for tracking. There are no advertising or analytics cookies on this website or in the application.

How long it is kept

  • Store and advertising data — for as long as your account exists, because the point of the product is to compare this year with last.
  • Request timings and error records — up to 90 days.
  • AI usage records — up to 400 days, for cost reporting.
  • Everything belonging to your account — deleted within 30 days of your account being closed.

Withdrawing access

You can disconnect Google at any time, either from Settings inside Ecomi or from your Google Account permissions page. When you do, the stored token is deleted and nothing further is read. The same applies to Shopify: remove the access token and the connection stops.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict what we do with it, or send it to you in a portable form. Write to support@ecomi.app and we will answer within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

About your customers' data

Order records from your Shopify store may contain your customers' details. Ecomi processes them only to produce your own sales and refund figures. They are not enriched, exported, sold or passed to anyone, and they remain governed by your own store's privacy policy. You remain the controller of that data; Ecomi acts on your instructions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we do with your data, we will update the date at the top and tell account holders by email before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data: support@ecomi.app
