Questions

The things people ask first

Who is Ecomi for?

Shopify shops that advertise on Google and import products from suppliers or competitors. If you run a catalogue that changes every week and you cannot tell which parts of it pay for themselves, that is the problem Ecomi was built for.

What does Ecomi do with my Google Ads account?

It reads cost, clicks, impressions and conversions, and the status of your Merchant Center listings. The connection is read-only: no bid, budget, campaign or listing is ever created, changed, paused or deleted. You can disconnect it at any time from Settings, or from your Google Account permissions page.

Why not just use the Google Ads reports?

Google knows what you spent but not what the goods cost you, and Shopify knows what you sold but not what it cost to sell it. Neither subtracts returns. Ecomi puts both on the same row and takes the cost of goods and the refunds off, which is the first figure that is actually profit.

Where does Market radar's data come from?

Public product and collection pages of the shops you choose to follow — the same pages anyone can open in a browser. Ecomi signs in nowhere, takes nothing from a theme or an admin, and collects no personal data from those shops.

Can I run more than one store?

Yes. Stores in different countries and currencies sit under one account and report in one currency, so three shops produce one set of figures rather than three.

What does it cost?

Pricing is being finalised. Write to support@ecomi.app and we will tell you where it stands.

How do I get my data deleted?

Ask at support@ecomi.app. Everything belonging to your account is deleted within 30 days of it being closed. The privacy policy sets out what is kept and for how long.
